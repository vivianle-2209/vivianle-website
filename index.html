A little more context

I like making complex things feel clear, useful, and considered.

My interest usually starts where ambiguity, systems, and people overlap. I am most engaged when I can turn scattered information into a structured direction, then shape it into something that feels elegant to use.

What drives me

I am drawn to work that begins messy. A vague problem, too many moving parts, unclear decisions, or data that does not yet say anything useful.

How I work

I usually move by finding the structure first, then refining the communication. I care about logic, but I also care about whether the outcome feels intuitive to someone else.

What I am building toward

The kind of work I want to keep doing sits between analytics, product thinking, and creative problem-solving. I like building systems that are both rigorous and human.
